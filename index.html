DNS filters only encrypt domain lookups—they do NOT hide your IP. Use ProtonVPN Free (unlimited data, strict no-logs, blocks P2P on free tier) or Cloudflare WARP (masks your residential IP for web & direct downloads).

Blocks ads, malware, and ISP DNS logging across your entire machine. Copy the DNS-over-HTTPS URL below into Chrome, Brave, or Firefox network settings:
